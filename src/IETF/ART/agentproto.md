# Agent Communication Protocols (agentproto)
* <IETFschedule meets=true>IETF127: Mon 16 Nov 2026 14:00 - Continental 5</IETFschedule>
* * [About AGENTPROTO](https://datatracker.ietf.org/group/agentproto/about)
* Potentially of interest because: Interoperability between different AI agents and potential privacy implications.
* Keywords: Agentic AI


When AI agents interact with their environment–other agents, tools, services, users–they do so through dialogues that consist out of all the messages that go back and forth.

To keep the dialog from getting mixed up with other dialogs, one needs to keep track of who is taking part in which dialogue. That involves the lifecycle of the dialogue (e.g. did we finish our conversation?) and the behavior of said dialogs during potential handover of tasks between the actors.

The working group aims to define a common way to manage these dialogs. This includes developing dialog identifiers and defining how information about a dialog is passed between participants.

Because dialog identifiers may persist across intermediaries and trust boundaries, they could create privacy risks. For example, an identifier might allow interactions that would otherwise appear unrelated to be linked together. The working group will analyze these privacy risks and specify mitigations.
