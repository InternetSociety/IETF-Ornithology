# Birds of a Feather (BoFs) 

In general, BoFs or Bird of a Feather meetings are interesting because they discuss potential new streams of work. It is the place where new work often surfaces. Information on BoFs can be found on the [IETF data tracker](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/bof-requests).[^note]

Here we enumerate all BoFs that we identified. We provide a high-level summary and indicate their relation to what *might* be policy interests. Readers are advised to follow the link in the title to gather more background about the BoF and find links to related Internet drafts and other prior art.

Often, before working groups are formed, meetings are organized to assess if there is enough clarity and interest around a particular issue to form a working group. 

At IETF126 there are no less than five BoFs scheduled. The first three of the BoFs in the enumeration below are inspired by the needs of Agentic AI (autonomous, adaptive intelligent software system that uses AI models to complete a specific task). For a pluriform multivendor, distributed architecture these will need to be able to communicate among themselves and other services; they will need to be able to discover services, and will need the network resources to do this at enormous scale. The agentic AI related BoFs deal with these type of topics. In general the existence of open scalable interoperable technologies in this space is a topic of public policy interest, the details in the work most probably do not.

* [Discovery of Agents, Workloads, and Named entities(dawn)](./dawn.md)
* [Agent Communication Protocols(agentproto)](./agentproto.md)
* [Dynamic Multi-agent Secured Collaboration(dmsc)](./dmsc.md)
* [Protocol for Transposed Transactions over HTTP(ptth)](./ptth.md)
* [Continuous Updating and Ratcheting for Rekeying Encrypted Network Transport(current)](./current.md)


[^note]: BoF comes from “Birds of a feather flock together” an English proverb, also the inspiration for the esoteric title of this document.
