# Email Address Verification (EVP)
* <IETFschedule meets=true>IETF127: Tue 17 Nov 2026 14:00 - Continental 6</IETFschedule>
* [BoF request](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/bofreq-hardt-email-address-verification/)
* Potentially of interest because: We consider this primarily a usability and security feature that reduces phishing potential and privacy exposure. We do not see fundamental public policy issues.
* Keywords: Usability, email validation.

During many service enrollment procedures, a user has to prove control of an email address, for example by copying a one-time code from an email or following a link. These methods can be annoying to users, are vulnerable to phishing, and can reveal to the email provider which service the user is signing up for.

This proposed working group sets out to simplify and automate proof of control of an email address while reducing disclosure of which service the user intends to use.
