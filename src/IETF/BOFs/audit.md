# Agent Use of Delegation and Interaction Traceabilitys (audit)
* <IETFschedule meets=true>IETF127: Tue 17 Nov 2026 09:00 - Continental 6</IETFschedule>
* [Group Information](https://datatracker.ietf.org/group/audit/about/)
* Potentially of interest because: we see no public policy issues.
* Keywords: mail processing, mail filtering, MTA extensibility
 
This is a working group forming BOF.

As software agents increasingly act on behalf of users across multiple services and administrative domains, it becomes harder to answer basic questions such as who initiated an action, under what authority, and how permissions changed along the way. This working-group-forming BoF will discuss interoperable mechanisms and data models for recording, exchanging, and verifying audit information across the full chain of interactions between users, agents, subagents, and tools, building on existing work such as OAuth, HTTP, and attestation and transparency frameworks. The BoF follows a side meeting at IETF 126.
